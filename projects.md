        <h1>Research Programs</h1>
        <p class="lead">Our lab investigates three core pillars of microbiome science to improve livestock health and sustainability.</p>
    </div>

    <article class="project" id="health">
        <div class="project-num">01</div>
        <div class="project-body">
            <h2>Microbiome &amp; Host Health</h2>
            <div class="callout">
                <p><strong>Goal:</strong> To understand how early-life microbial colonization shapes the immune system in swine and ruminants.</p>
            </div>
            <p>We are investigating:</p>
            <ul>
                <li>The "window of opportunity" for gut colonization in piglets.</li>
                <li>How maternal diet influences the offspring's microbiome (maternal imprinting).</li>
                <li>Development of synbiotics (probiotics + prebiotics) to reduce scouring and improve weaning transitions.</li>
            </ul>
        </div>
    </article>

    <article class="project" id="amr">
        <div class="project-num">02</div>
        <div class="project-body">
            <h2>Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR)</h2>
            <div class="callout">
                <p><strong>Goal:</strong> To map and mitigate the spread of antimicrobial resistance genes (ARGs) in livestock environments.</p>
            </div>
            <p>We use metagenomics to study:</p>
            <ul>
                <li>The "resistome" of cattle and swine manure.</li>
                <li>How manure treatment methods (composting, anaerobic digestion) affect ARG persistence.</li>
                <li>The role of plasmids in transferring resistance between environmental and pathogenic bacteria.</li>
            </ul>
        </div>
    </article>

    <article class="project" id="sustainability">
        <div class="project-num">03</div>
        <div class="project-body">
            <h2>Sustainability &amp; Methane Mitigation</h2>
            <div class="callout">
                <p><strong>Goal:</strong> To reduce the environmental footprint of livestock production.</p>
            </div>
            <p>Current projects include:</p>
            <ul>
                <li>Characterizing the rumen microbiome of high- vs. low-methane emitting cattle.</li>
                <li>Testing feed additives (e.g., biochar, tannins) that inhibit methanogens without hurting digestion.</li>
                <li>Using multi-omics to discover novel biomarkers for feed efficiency.</li>
            </ul>

            <div class="sub-project">
                <span class="eyebrow">Featured resource</span>
                <h3>Rumen Biobank Construction</h3>
                <p>As a key component of our mitigation strategy, we are isolating and characterizing novel rumen microbes to build a comprehensive biobank of strains and genomes.</p>
