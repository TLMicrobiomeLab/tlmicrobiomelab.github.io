            <p class="muted small">Summer research &amp; honours theses</p>
            <p>We offer opportunities for undergraduate summer research awards (USRA) and honours thesis projects. This is a great way to gain hands-on experience in:</p>

                <li>Standard molecular biology techniques</li>
                <li>Next-generation sequencing technologies</li>
